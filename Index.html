The Actives

Ingredients that earn
their place.

Matrixyl 3000

Peptide Complex · Anti-Aging

A clinically proven peptide blend of Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1 and Tetrapeptide-7 that signals skin to produce more collagen and elastin, visibly reducing wrinkles over time.

Argireline

Neuro-Peptide · Tightening

Acetyl Hexapeptide-3 works by relaxing facial muscle contractions — the same mechanism that causes expression lines — without injectables or downtime.

Hyaluronic Acid

Humectant · Plumping

Multi-molecular weight hyaluronic acid hydrates at multiple skin depths simultaneously — low MW penetrates deeper layers, high MW works at the surface for immediate plumping.

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3 · Multi-Corrector

One of the most versatile skincare actives known to science. Minimizes pores, fades hyperpigmentation, regulates sebum, and strengthens the skin's barrier function.

Alpha-Arbutin

Brightener · Dark Spot Corrector

A stable, skin-safe derivative that inhibits tyrosinase — the enzyme responsible for melanin production — to fade existing dark spots and prevent new ones from forming.

Ascorbyl Glucoside

Stable Vitamin C · Antioxidant

A next-generation Vitamin C derivative that is significantly more stable than pure ascorbic acid, converting to active Vitamin C in the skin for potent antioxidant and brightening action.

Squalane

Emollient · Barrier Support

A skin-identical lipid derived from olives that mimics the skin's own sebum. Lightweight, non-comedogenic, and profoundly nourishing without greasiness.

Panthenol (B5)

Soothing · Repair

Pro-Vitamin B5 penetrates deeply into the skin, converting to pantothenic acid. It promotes cellular repair, soothes irritation, and enhances moisture retention.

Encapsulated Retinol

Vitamin A · Cell Renewal

Retinol encapsulated in a time-release delivery system for dramatically reduced irritation compared to standard retinol, while maintaining full efficacy for cellular renewal and collagen synthesis.
